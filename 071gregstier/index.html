Missions Pulse 71: Greg Stier

October 31, 2021

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Greg Stier, Founder/Visionary of Dare 2 Share Ministries. Greg Stier is a champion for unleashing this generation with the Gospel. As the founder of Dare 2 Share Ministries, he is driven to help the Church activate Christian teenagers to reach their friends. In the last 30 years, Greg has trained millions of youth leaders and students on how to relationally engage their world with the Good News of Jesus. A much sought-after speaker, Greg is a former pastor, church planter, youth leader, as well as the author of numerous books, including his latest, Unlikely Fighter: The Story of How a Fatherless Street Kid Overcame Violence, Chaos, and Confusion to Become a Radical Christ Follower.

Greg Stier talks about every teen everywhere hearing the gospel from a friend, the post-Christian landscape of this generation, Gen Z: the sleeping giant of the church, gospelizing your youth ministry, prayer, evangelism, and disciple-making, preaching the gospel with words, Life in 6 Words, and his new book, Unlikely Fighter.
