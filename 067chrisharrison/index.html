Missions Pulse 67: Chris Harrison

April 30, 2020

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Chris Harrison, president of Agape International, which plants Bible schools and churches across Southeast Asia.

Chris talks about whether or not coronavirus can cancel God's mission, did God send COVID-19?, a crazy, near-death experience in Thailand, and a massive revival is on the horizon.

Make sure to subscribe on YouTube, iTunes, Google Podcasts, or Stitcher. 

On this episode of Missions Pulse, @davidjoannes interviews @charrison212, president of Agape International, revivalist, former pastor, and long-term missionary in Thailand. #missionspulse

Chris Harrison shares his incredible near-death experience. After his many overseas mission trips throughout the years, he never had a problem with his health. Until late 2019. You might not believe this story, but he has all the doctors' and medical reports. Get ready for your faith to strengthen after you hear this testimony of God's healing and His passion for the nations.
