Missions Pulse 62: Wayne Tingle

March 23, 2020

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Wayne Tingle, founder of Enrich Missions, missionary coach and counselor, and long-term missionary to Nicaragua.

Wayne talks about being a contractor to becoming a missionary, the need for righteous deviants, does God qualify the called or call the qualified?, advance Kingdom emissaries, and radical love that changes the world.

Make sure to subscribe on YouTube, iTunes, Google Podcasts, or Stitcher.

Wayne Tingle was living the good life when God catapulted him in a different direction. H tells his story of being a contractor to becoming a missionary. He advocates for run-of-the-mill, ordinary Christians to walk into the call of God.

Wayne Tingle says that his rebellious nature leads to Kingdom opportunities. He swims against the current of culture, looking for opportunities to extend the Kingdom. We need more righteous deviants to think and act outside the box.

It takes a generation rallied together to create change. That's what keeps Wayne Tingle going. His underlying motivation is a deep-seated love for people that God instilled in his heart.

There's been a lot of debate whether God calls the qualified or qualifies the called. The reality is there's a lot of space in foreign missions for you, regardless of who you are.
