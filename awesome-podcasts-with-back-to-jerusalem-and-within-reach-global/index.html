Awesome Podcasts with Back to Jerusalem and Within Reach Global

May 31, 2016

Back to Jerusalem and Within Reach Global are having fun tag teamin’ each other on back to back podcasts and live video broadcasts!

Awesome missions podcasts: @TheOfficialBTJ & @within_reach tag teamin’ each other!

It’s been an honor to connect with Eugene Bach of Back to Jerusalem, swapping stories of revival, persecution, unreached peoples and the final frontier of the mission field along the Silk Road.

Eugene struck first, interviewing me in this shoddy, makeshift podcast from my cellphone in China. He said I sounded robotic. I thought it sounded epic.

Check out the Back to Jerusalem Podcast, Episode 71: "We’ve got David Joannes on the line with us today. He’s the Founder and President of Within Reach Global and he’s been in Southwest China for about 17 years doing great things in the ministry. He stays very busy with a multitude of activities and teams within his organization. Listen to him share what he’s up to!" (28:27)

You can listen to the podcast directly here, but we encourage you to subscribe to the Back to Jerusalem Podcast on iTunes for upcoming conversations.

A couple weeks later, Back to Jerusalem missionaries were in Thailand. We connected over pineapple fried rice, jumbo prawns and fresh coconuts. Then we did another spontaneous podcast in the sweltering heat and humidity. It was an exciting conversation.

Check out the Back to Jerusalem Podcast, Episode 74: "We are back with David Joannes, founder of Within Reach Global, and this time in person! Eugene and David have been working with some Chinese partners in Thailand and stopped to share some information and news with you on this podcast." (21:02)

You can listen to the podcast directly here, but we encourage you to subscribe to the Back to Jerusalem Podcast on iTunes for upcoming conversations.

Now it was my turn! We had Eugene on the Missions Blab for a live video broadcast. It was a powerful conversation about China’s vision to reach the remaining unreached people groups along the Silk Road, as well as The Heavenly Man, Brother Yun’s upcoming UK tour.

#Missions #Blab @TheOfficialBTJ talk about #revival, #unreached, #persecution + more!

Watch the replay here

You’ll probably want to subscribe to upcoming conversations on the Missions Blab. There’s a bunch of great stuff on the burner.

Hope you’re inspired by these conversations. The whole goal to get more people on board with us to reach the unreached. This is a body of Christ ordeal. Stay in the loop. Find out what God is up to in Asia. Join Him in this holy mandate to finish the Great Commission task!
