Missions Pulse 12: Ron Luce

March 25, 2019

The Christian life is not a solo venture; it's a relay race. Ron Luce says, "It doesn't matter how fast you run. If you don't pass the baton to the next generation, you don't win."

In this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Ron Luce, founder of Teen Mania Ministries and Jesus Global Youth Day. We talk about the importance of sowing into the next generation of young people. How is Ron doing this? For the last few years, he has been traveling the world, connecting movement leaders and pastors for the first ever Jesus Global Youth Day event taking place in Manila, Philippines on August 8-10, 2019.

Ron Luce played a pivotal role in thrusting out over 80,000 youth on overseas missions. Through Acquire the Fire youth conferences, he inspired millions of people to seek God for their specific role in God's Kingdom. His mantra to leaders today is simple: "You make sure that when you die, the baton of the Gospel is safely in the hands of the next generation."

In this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews @ronluce, founder of Teen Mania Ministries and @JesusGYD, which takes place in the Manila, Philippines on August 8-10,2019. #missionspulse
