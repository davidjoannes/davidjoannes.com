Missions Pulse 73: Leo Bigger

November 30, 2021

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Leo Bigger, Pastor of ICF Church (Zürich) and leader of ICF Movement. 

Pastor Leo talks about the explosive effects of following God's will, local church as the place where you practice your gifts and talents, reliance on the supernatural swinging the pendulum of post-Christian cultures, and the spiritual hunger of young Europeans.
