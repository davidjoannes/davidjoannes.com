A lack of gospel access is the greatest injustice known to humanity. So we go where no one else is going.

42% of the world's population lives in the 10/40 Window with little or no access to the gospel.

Since 1997, Within Reach Global has focused all of its energy on unreached people groups, so that every nation, tribe, people, and language will worship before God's throne.

From the field

The gospel is reaching the unreached

In remote villages across Thailand and Myanmar, local missionaries trained through Within Reach Global are planting churches and discipling new believers. What began with a small team has multiplied into a movement of indigenous leaders carrying the gospel to their own communities.

"We trailblaze to places where there is no presence of the gospel. That is where the harvest is."

David Joannes, Founder & CEO
