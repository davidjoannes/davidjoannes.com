How Christians Unwittingly Prevent Gospel Access

September 24, 2022

I recently joined Mike Falkenstine and Steve Schirmer on Missions Talk to talk about how Christians unwittingly prevent gospel access to the unreached.

This episode is based on a story from the book Hudson Taylor's Spiritual Secret.

In the book, a new Chinese convert asks Taylor, “How long have you had the glad tidings (the gospel) in your country?”

“Some hundreds of years,” was the reluctant reply.

“What! Hundreds of years?” the Chinese man said. “My father sought the Truth,” he continued sadly, “and died without finding it. Oh, why did you not come sooner?”

It was a moment, the pain of which Hudson Taylor could never forget and which deepened his earnestness in seeking to bring Christ to those who might still be reached.

In this episode, we focus on the reality of 2.1 billion who have never heard the gospel once and the 3.3 billion who are in an unreached people group (according to Joshua Project). The Great Commission calls us to proclaim the gospel and make disciples. The problem is so few Christians have the conviction and determination to obey the commands Jesus gave us.
