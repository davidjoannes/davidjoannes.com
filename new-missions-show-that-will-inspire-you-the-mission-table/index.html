New Missions Show That Will Inspire You: The Mission Table

February 6, 2015

I recently came across a website called The Mission Table produced by Sixteen:Fifteen, and I have to say, I am super excited about seeing how it turns out. A bunch of missionally-inspired people sitting around a table, conversing about valid missions issues? Yea, that’s my kind of show.

I really like that their first episode is called Everyone Is A Missionary, stated (I hope) in a facetious manner, because I could not disagree more! Here’s why:

Just Because You’re A Christian, Doesn’t Mean You’re A Missionary

The first episode airs February 17, 2015. I’ll be there watching. Maybe even taking part in the Live Webinar on March 3, 2015.

What about you? Want to learn more about missions? Want to challenge yourself and those around you by rethinking some missions issues?
