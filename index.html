About David

From Bible smuggler to mission leader.

David began his missionary journey by smuggling Bibles into China. After serving for 15 years in Southwest China, he now lives in Chiang Mai, Thailand, with his wife, Lorna, and their two daughters.

He has been interrogated for his faith over 20 times and has been apprehended by dozens of child soldiers carrying AK-47s. Today he leads Within Reach Global, hosts the Missions Pulse podcast, and writes to help the Church see the unreached.
