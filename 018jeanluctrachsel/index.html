Missions Pulse 18: Jean-Luc Trachsel

May 6, 2019

You've heard the rhetoric for years. They say that Europe is a post-Christian society; that the Great Awakenings of the past are just that: a historical phenomenon.

So where does that leave us today? Can God do today what He did historically in Europe? If yes, then will He? What is our role to play in the revivals to come? Do our efforts have any bearing on the lives of those around us?

In this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Jean-Luc Trachsel, the international director of International Association of Healing Ministries (IAHM) and the primary vision carrier for Europe Shall Be Saved. Jean-Luc is witnessing a modern-day revival in Europe firsthand. Though he recognizes the prevalent cultural trends of a shrinking Christian population in Europe, he is seeing incredible instances of God's power at work in anomalous areas.

Prepare for a new paradigm as you join this conversation entitled Why Europe Shall Be Saved.
