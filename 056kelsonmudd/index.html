Missions Pulse 56: Kelson Mudd

February 10, 2020

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Kelson Mudd, missionary and entrepreneur, CEO of Klink Fundraising, squad member of GlobalU, son of Adventures in Missions and World Race president, and host of The Kelson Mudd Show.

Kelson Mudd talks about young people transforming mission, new missions tech, Klink Fundraising, the broad scope of missional outreach, thriving on mission wherever you are, God’s ever-present grace, and when what you’ve been taught as a kid becomes a real faith.

Make sure to subscribe on YouTube, iTunes, Google Podcasts, or Stitcher.

Stop thinking that young people can't change the world! Myriads of transformative tech and innovation have been birthed in the minds of youth. Kelson Mudd talks about Klink Fundraising, an incredible new app that connects missionaries to donors in a natural and relational way, helping them to fundraise more efficiently.

Francis Chan talks about the nearby ponds that few are fishing in. His challenge is that we move beyond the norm and see the harvest fields on the horizon. Kelson Mudd wanted to see a different aspect of the Church. His overseas missions experience revealed the broader scope of God's mission narrative.

Is God's call for every Christian? Kelson Mudd says missional living starts with being filled. The motto of Missions Pulse stands true: When you know God's heart it is natural to join God's mission. This obedience comes through brokenness and allowing God to repair the broken bits. Die to self and allow the all-surpassing power of God to flow through your cracked jar of clay. Hope is always there.

When you grow up in a Christian home, the things you're taught have a tendency to become cliché. But God's grace is always present. Kelson Mudd says that stepping out of your comfort zone and beyond your barriers often makes things real. When you say "yes" to the Lord, it makes the ordinary extraordinary.
