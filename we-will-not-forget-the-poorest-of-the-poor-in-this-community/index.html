We Will Not Forget The Poorest Of The Poor In This Community

November 24, 2015

The core of the gospel is life on life. It's a sharing of the glory of God to every human being.

The core of the gospel is life on life. It's a sharing of the glory of God to every human.

The problem? There are millions who have yet to hear of the name of Jesus—even once.

That's why at Within Reach Global we are passionate about taking the holistic gospel to people who have not heard it before.

Here's a beautiful community of people around The Edge outreach center who we are blessing in a tangible way.

Clothing, bedsheets and basic necessities have been donated by underground churches throughout China, and sent to our local missionaries to give to these unreached tribes in Myanmar.
