Within Reach Global

Extending the Church to Where the Church Is Not, at Least Not Yet

September 17, 2014

The ministry of Within Reach Global is continually growing!

More volunteer missionaries.
More visiting teams.
More life impact.

We are excited about the growth and partnerships being formed, and have recently been accredited by a few quality organizations. The Issachar Initiative is one of them.

Within Reach Global is now recognized as a Count For Zero organization.

What does that mean? It means that we are part of a group of organizations that is pooling our resources and efforts to reach the least reach people groups on the planet, specifically in the 10/40 Window.

We focus on Where The Zeros Are:

Zero written scripture.
Zero churches.
Zero disciples.
Zero missionaries.
Zero chance to hear the gospel.

99.7% of missional activities and financial support are directed toward where the church ALREADY IS.
Only 0.3% of resources are allocated to where the church is NOT.

97% missions $ sent where the church ALREADY IS. 0.3% sent where it's NOT.

As we see the Great Imbalance of Global Missions, Within Reach Global is committed to projects focused on three of the “currently very strategic” elements of the Great Commission:

Disciple Makers to UUPG’s (unreached unengaged people groups)
Evangelism
Church Planting and Presence

It’s time the global Church recognizes the final frontier of missions among the unreached and unengaged people groups of the 10/40 Window.

It’s time the global Church recognizes the final frontier of missions among the unreached.

Will you stand with us in prayer and financial support as we bring the gospel to where the Church is not… at least not yet?
