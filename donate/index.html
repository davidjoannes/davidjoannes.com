Partner with the Joannes family

A family on mission, serving the unreached for 30 years.

David and Lorna are the founders of Within Reach Global and full-time missionaries in Chiang Mai, Thailand, with their two daughters. For three decades they have planted churches, discipled leaders, and built a movement that keeps multiplying.
