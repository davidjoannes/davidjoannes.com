Joshua Project: Highlighting The Unreached [Video]

December 20, 2015

I asked Duane Frasier, researcher at Joshua Project, this question: "Do missions data, numbers and statistics really matter? How do they make a difference?” He did a great job answering, but for me it was a rhetorical question.

@joshuaproject Do missions data/numbers/statistics matter? Do they make a difference?

I have seen firsthand how the gathering of all this missions data of unreached people groups' history, culture, location and percentages of Christian breakthrough has directly impacted both my personal missionary venture as well as Within Reach Global, the ministry God has graciously given me.

In short, Joshua Project has changed my life. That’s why it was such an honor to interview the experts during a live video broadcast entitled, Joshua Project: Highlighting The Unreached.
