#108: The Undomesticated Missionary: Faith, Family, and Following God Anywhere

May 15, 2026

Joshua Shively is the director of short-term mission trips and internship at Within Reach Global. Josh talks about his family's move from California to Thailand and how they're seeing God move in and through their missional efforts.
