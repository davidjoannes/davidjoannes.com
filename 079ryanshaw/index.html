Missions Pulse episode 79: Ryan Shaw

February 28, 2022

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Ryan Shaw, President/International Lead Facilitator, Global Mission Mobilization Initiative (GMMI).

Shaw talks about his motivation for writing his new book Rethinking Global Mobilization, recalibrating the meaning of mission mobilization, how local churches can practically implement a mobilization framework, and mission mobilization as the core identity of the church.

The Space Between Memories highlights the last two decades of David Joannes’ missionary travels. From the hullabaloo of Manila to the jam-packed city streets of Kunming to the rural villages of Southwest China, this part memoir, part travelogue shows God’s hand at work among the most unassuming of misfits. It is a testament that if only we would answer his call, his glory can be found, even in the space between memories.
