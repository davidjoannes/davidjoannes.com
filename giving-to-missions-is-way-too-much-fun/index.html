All These Ways To Donate Make Giving To Missions Way Too Much Fun!

April 27, 2014

We are so blessed by all the people who have stood behind us for many years as we at Within Reach Global endeavor to reach unreached people groups in Southeast Asia and the 10/40 Window.

Now we want to honor you by making it even easier to donate to the cause of bringing the gospel to the unreached. If people can’t click one button to give, then many will just give up without donating to a worthy ministry. That's why we've made it more fun to give.

"No one has ever become poor by giving." - Anne Frank

I’d like to introduce the all new Donation page on davidjoannes.com! There’s a bunch of easy ways to give. Here’s some of the options:

You can now donate using your credit card! No need to sign up for Paypal or third party platforms. Just enter your credit card info and press submit. Plus, you can select “One Time Donation” or automatically give a “Monthly Donation” without returning to my website every month. You’re welcome!

Of course, if Paypal is easier for you, you can still make one time or monthly donations straight from my site. Select your amount, login, and send. It’s that easy.

The "Other Ways To Give” link is fully of goodies!

The most popular one is, of course, old school donations by check. If you’d like to designate specifically to the Joannes family, just write “Joannes” in the memo, then send to our mailing address: Within Reach Global, P.O. Box 1591, Prescott, AZ 86302, USA.

"Donate While You Shop" by using Amazon Smile and Amazon will give 0.5% of your purchase price to Within Reach Global. You simply purchase your products online and don’t feel a thing. Set your Amazon page so that all your future purchases will benefit Within Reach Global. It’s the perfect painless way to help us reach unreached people groups.

For all you super geeks out there, you can now donate Bitcoin on our Bitpay account! If you don't know what Bitcoin is, disregard this. For techies, no need for instructions. You know what to do!

Remember, all donations made to Within Reach Global are tax deductible. Receipts of your giving will be mailed to you at the end of the year.

Thank you so much for standing with us as we bring the gospel of Jesus Christ to the farthest regions of the unreached world. May God bless you back one hundred fold as you give generously!
