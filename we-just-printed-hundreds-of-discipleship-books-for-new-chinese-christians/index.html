We Just Printed Hundreds Of Discipleship Books For New Chinese Christians

June 18, 2015

There is something stirring in China. It has been going on for a while now. The whispers of continued revival. The results of missionary seeds and faithful prayers of the saints.
There is a spiritual vacuum in China. In post Cultural Revolution China, people are longing for spiritual truth. “You just kick the tree and the fruit is falling to the ground! That’s how ripe it is.” That’s how some missionaries describe it.
“You just kick the tree and the fruit is falling to the ground! That’s how ripe it is.”
We are seeing many new young Chinese college students and rural tribal people getting saved. Our present task is to disciple them, to help them grow in their new Christian faith.
“I have been an atheist all my life,” a Chinese college student just told us. “But when you tell me about this God of yours, something tells me that it is real."
"When you tell me about this God of yours, something tells me that it is real.” - Chinese atheist
We just printed 300 of each of our 3 part book curriculum, a total of 900 new books that are already being used to train a new generation of believers in China! We are excited about the growth we are seeing. Please continue to pray that the spiritual vacuum in China will be filled by the presence of Jesus.
