Missions Pulse 72: Zach & Megan Duke

November 8, 2021

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Zach and Megan Duke, Worship Leader/Global Leaders at Biglife. Zach and Megan are the Global Leaders of Biglife, National leaders at the 24:14 Coalition, and on the leadership team at Zume Training. Additionally, Megan Duke is a sought-after worship leader who has led worship for multiple organizations at major Christian events throughout the world. 

Zach and Megan talk about the responsibility of joining Jesus on mission, how disciples and churches start in the home, how missions is a family affair, how we ought to see color and distinctions in racial diversity, and why so many Christians stop short of faith-sharing after they’ve experienced God in a meaningful way.
