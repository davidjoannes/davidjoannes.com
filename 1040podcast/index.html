1040 Podcast: Gospel Privilege and the Unreached

October 12, 2021

I was honored to spend time with 1040 Podcast host Sarah Murphy as we explored the concept of gospel privilege and gospel responsibility. The topic was inspired by my recent book with the same title, Gospel Privilege: The Unearned Advantage That's Meant for Everyone, available on Amazon.

This conversation, entitled Gospel Privilege + The Unreached, first appeared on 1040 Podcast on September 28, 2021. 

Sarah Murphy, host of @the1040podcast sponsored by @the_gofund, interviews @davidjoannes about Gospel Privilege + The Unreached. Click to listen!
