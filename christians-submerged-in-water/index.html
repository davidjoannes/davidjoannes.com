New Chinese Christians Transform After Being Submerged Beneath 15 Inches Of Water

December 3, 2013

Our little vision of a college student ministry is budding into a center for salvation and revival!

Every week, The Bridge and The Hub student centers see over 200 Chinese college students come from all over the country. Most of them have never heard of the gospel before. But as we invest into their lives, there are a number of them who have already surrendered their lives to God, and are now being discipled. Our team is committed to following up, caring for them, loving them, and helping them walk victoriously in their new Christian life.

See photos of this incredible time on Facebook!

In November, we started the Good News Series with 15 students, a great discipleship material that helps them build the foundation of their faith. Six of them were baptized in water, and everyone accepted the baptism of the Holy Spirit at the same time inside a small, unassuming apartment living room. Many of them came up out of the blow-up pool baptismal water clapping and worshipping! It was special to watch this life transformation.
