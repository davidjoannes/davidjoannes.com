Missions Pulse 042: Bevin Ginder

October 28, 2019

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Bevin Ginder, founder of Globalcast Resources, mentoring missions advocates for Great Commission efforts.

Bevin talks about the powerful role of missions mobilizers, how we must make the mission of God central in all our Christian activities, and how the ripples of our obedience extend far and wide beyond our understanding.

On this episode of Missions Pulse, @davidjoannes interviews Bevin Ginder @Gindertribe, founder of @globalcast, mentoring missions advocates for Great Commission efforts. #missionspulse

It's so clear that there is so much to do, but many times there is no one to trumpet the missions call. We must recognize that the people in New York and Nebraska are as lost as those who live in Nairobi and Nepal. The difference? Many beyond our borders have no access to the Gospel message. They are Gospel-deprived. Thus the need for the missions mobilizer. Learn more from Bevin Ginder here.

Bevin Ginder talks about the openness toward the Gospel in Cambodia. He gives a backdrop of YWAM's early mercy ministry that began and led to the growth of the Cambodian Church. Small moments trigger transformative momentum. "The choices we make today echo in eternity." How are you creating ripples for eternity today?

Bevin Ginder says that as we pray we catch the heart of God. How many times does God ask us to be the answer to our prayers but we don't respond? Missions motivation must flow from intimacy with Christ. How do you begin? Start in prayer. He'll deposit into your life compassion for the lost that only flows after spending time with Jesus. Read Psalm 37:4 to understand more.
