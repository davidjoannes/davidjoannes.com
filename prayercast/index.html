Global Missions Amidst a Pandemic: A Live Prayercast

COVID-19 changed everything. But God hasn't changed and the mission isn't canceled! Check out Global Missions Amidst a Pandemic: A Live Prayercast, which aired May 1, 2020. Watch the replay now! #MissionsPulse.

Amidst the many priorities of Christian churches worldwide, global missions is generally on the low end of the spectrum. It’s hard enough to maintain a focus on missions when times are easy. Enter a worldwide pandemic, and international missions efforts can quickly get swept deeper under the rug.

COVID-19 changed everything.

Pastors and leaders seek to gather their members into online meetings, small groups join Zoom conference calls, and church giving is down. But Jesus still beckons us to spread abroad His grace and glory—in our zip codes, our cities, nearby states, neighboring nations, and to the ends of the earth.

Our goal is to highlight God’s macro narrative and bring global missions to the forefront of Christian conversation.

God has not changed and the mission is not canceled!

Moments like these cause us to pause and consider the Psalm 46:10 mandate: “Be still and know that I am God.” But Heaven forbid we overlook the second part of the same verse: "I will be exalted in the nations. I will be exalted in the earth.”

It’s time to poise in a posture of expectancy and prepare for the harvest on the horizon. All of history is moving toward the grand culmination of the ages as seen in Revelation 7:9—every nation, tribe, people, and language, standing before God’s throne, knees bent in worship and adoration.

We pray that this global prayercast will trigger a passion for God’s glory and for His overarching plan to redeem humankind.
