A Glimpse Into Local Missionary Ministry Acts 29 Style

November 6, 2012

Translated from an email sent by Brother Hu, Within Reach Global local missionary.

These are the some of the daily ministry outreach our local missionaries are doing at 26 Tribe outreach center:

See Facebook photos here.

Preaching to people who've never heard of Jesus
Praying for the sick and diseased
Helping and taking care of the elderly
Praying for sick people with rheumatism, stomach problems, psoriasis skin disease, heart failures, ear problems and many others.

Serious Acts 29 kinda stuff!

Also, ministry among elderly monks continues as they help provide clothing and food for them. The monks are living in extreme poverty, trying to find their way to salvation. Our underground missionaries are stepping in to a place in their lives where they are open to hear about true salvation.

Monk ministry. Yea, that’s our kinda thing right there!

Pray for our ministry to Buddhist monks!

They are also ministering among the young people in the city at 26 Tribes, and leading house church meetings every week.

Please pray for them!
