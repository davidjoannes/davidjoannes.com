101 Good Christian Missions Books for Your Viewing Pleasure

February 9, 2015

I’m always on the search for a great missions book.

Unfortunately—and incredibly surprising to a guy like me who is absolutely passionate about missions stories—A good missionary book is tough to find.

Go into your local Christian bookstore and look for books on missions. First of all, there is no missions section! What? It’s only Jesus’ final command, the Great Commission, the charge he left us with before returning to heaven. When you finally do stumble upon a missions book tucked away in the far corner of the Christian bookstore, you’re left with a great story, but told in a poorly articulated fashion.

That makes me sad. Maybe I'll do something about it...

Missions work in cross cultural scenarios are some of the most exciting stories you will ever read. But Christian “self help” books are much more in fashion than the missionary story.

Missions work in cross cultural scenarios are some of the most exciting stories you will ever read.

Remember this: it’s the Great Commission, not the Great Suggestion.

One of my personal favorites, and strangely missing from this list, is the story of James O. Fraser, Mountain Rain by Eileen Crossman.

Have you read any of these books? Which one is next on your list? What’s your favorite missions book?

Note: Stay tuned for my book, coming out hopefully sooner than later! I’m in the process of writing it now.
