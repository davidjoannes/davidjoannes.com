A List Of Our Roles On The Mission Field

October 20, 2013

Have you ever wondered, "What do David and Lorna Joannes really do?"   

Well, here's a few of our roles and a list of things we're doing on the mission field.

Ministry efforts that we focus on:



  • Overseeing Within Reach Global ministries

  • Financial accounting for ministry donations

  • Vision casting for pioneering new outreach centers

  • Writing and blogging news updates from the mission field

  • Fundraising for our many ministry projects and outreaches

  • Growth and expansion of our local and foreign missionaries 

  • Evangelizing, teaching, mentoring and discipling new believers 

  • Providing quality Christian curriculum for training new believers

  • Building partnerships with other ministries to reach the unreached

  • Training underground Chinese Christians to reach their own people groups

  • Organizing and hosting underground leadership trainings among pastors in China

  • Ministry outreach among hundreds of college students at The Hub and The Bridge

  • Ministry outreach among unreached people groups at Pilot, The Edge, 26 Tribes and Bamboo

  • Creating films in English and Chinese to help inspire missions efforts in the 10/40 Window


Business requirements:



  • Social Media and marketing at ActsCo Printing (where we get our visa for Thailand)


Other ministries:



  • Teaching missionaries how to fundraise and tell their stories better

  • Blogging stories from the mission field to inspire Great Commission efforts

  • Helping missionaries and missions organizations with social media and marketing

  • Sharing the gospel with our neighbors


Things we do to subsidize low personal support:



  • Creating films for non profit organizations

  • Website development 

  • Social media and marketing 


Why do we do what we do?


Why do we do all this? Because we have seen the Great Imbalance of Global Missions, and it crushes us to know that there are over 2 billion yet to hear the name of Jesus.

We are passionate about bringing the gospel to people in the 10/40 Window who have yet to have a chance to know the life transforming power of Christ.
