Full bio

David Joannes is the Founder and CEO of Within Reach Global, which serves to give gospel access to unreached communities in the spiritual dead zones of Southeast Asia. Through compassion ministries, evangelism, discipleship, and church planting, the ministry has seen 85 churches planted among hard-to-reach animist, Islamic, atheist, and Buddhist peoples. David began his missionary journey by smuggling Bibles into China. He has been interrogated for his faith over 20 times and was once apprehended by dozens of child soldiers carrying AK-47s. He has eaten meals with headhunters, shared the gospel with tribal witchdoctors, witnessed secret baptisms, and taught at underground Bible trainings. After serving for 15 years in Southwest China, David has lived for more than 10 years in Chiang Mai, Thailand, where he oversees a growing team of local and foreign missionaries. He is fluent in Mandarin Chinese and proficient in Tagalog and Thai. He is the author of The Mind of a Missionary, The Mind of a Missionary Devotional, The Space Between Memories, Gospel Privilege, See the City, and Glorious Hope, and the host of the Missions Pulse podcast. He extends gospel access to unreached peoples with his wife, Lorna, and their two daughters.

Talking points

What David loves to talk about

1

From Bible smuggler to mission leader

How a teenager carrying Bibles into China ended up leading a mission organization, and what interrogations, close calls, and three decades on the field have taught him.

2

The mind of a missionary

What motivates missionaries, what they expect, what they sacrifice, and what rewards they find, drawn from the lives of Jim and Elisabeth Elliot, C. T. Studd, Hudson Taylor, Amy Carmichael, and others.

3

Gospel privilege and the access gap

Over 2 billion people have yet to hear the name of Jesus. Why access to the gospel is an unearned advantage, and what the Church can do about the imbalance.

4

Why local missionaries matter

How Within Reach Global raises up Thai and Asian missionaries to plant churches among their own neighbors, and why that reaches places outsiders cannot.

5

Finding your role in the Great Commission

Every believer has a part: pray, give, or go. Practical first steps for listeners, including free tools such as a spiritual gifts test and the Gospel Access Score.

6

Hope for the broken-hearted

The story of White Flower, a young war refugee from Myanmar, and the hope of the city of God in a world full of suffering.

7

Raising the next generation for missions

How to stir a heart for the nations in kids and teenagers, and why David created youth and kids editions of his missions course.

Suggested questions

Questions for podcasters and interviews

Choose seven to ten questions from this list, and feel free to ask your own as well.

  • You started out smuggling Bibles into China. How did that begin, and what was it like?
  • You have been interrogated for your faith more than 20 times. What goes through your mind in those moments?
  • Tell us about the day you were apprehended by dozens of child soldiers.
  • What does the term "unreached" mean, and why does it still matter today?
  • You write about "gospel privilege." What is it, and how should it change the way Christians live?
  • In The Mind of a Missionary you study well-known missionaries. Which one has shaped you the most, and why?
  • What do people most often misunderstand about missionaries?
  • Why does Within Reach Global focus on raising up local missionaries?
  • What does a secret baptism or an underground Bible training look like?
  • How have you and Lorna raised a family on the mission field for so many years?
  • What have three decades in Asia taught you about suffering and hope?
  • Not everyone is called to move overseas. How can an ordinary believer take part in the Great Commission?
  • What would you say to a young person who senses a call to missions?
  • How can our listeners pray for you and for Within Reach Global?

Within Reach Global

Since 1997, Within Reach Global has brought gospel access to unreached people groups in the 10/40 Window through four areas of work: compassion ministries, evangelism, discipleship, and church planting. Headquartered in Chiang Mai, Thailand, its team of local and foreign missionaries serves communities across Southeast Asia.
