Media In Missions: Multiplying God's Message Across Cultures [Video]

December 17, 2015

It was a pleasure to converse with Bill Snider, founder and director of Asia Pacific Media, on the topic of media in missions.

Our internet connection from Thailand to the Philippines was a bit shoddy after a nationwide Globe internet outage, but we pushed through and did the best we could to inspire people with a media background to use their skills

"The contextualized message of the gospel, when it is seen through the lens of a culture to a specific people or people group, that’s when you begin to see acceptance of the gospel and true transformation of culture.” - David Joannes

Want to be inspired to use your media skills to fulfill the Great Commission cross culturally? Watch the replay of our conversation, Media In Missions: Multiplying God's Message Across Cultures.

All my live video broadcast replays can be found on my Blab profile here.

Follow me on Blab for upcoming live video broadcasts.

“Missions is all about relationships. It’s about allowing those relationships to shape the embryonic idea that I or someone else might bring to the table, because media has got to be owned within the culture. If the gospel message is going to be contextualized, it’s all about relationships.” Bill Snider
