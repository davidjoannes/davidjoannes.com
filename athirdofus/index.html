A Third of Us: A Passion to Reach the Lost

November 14, 2021

The Alliance for the Unreached seeks to catalyze a movement that unites churches, organizations, and individuals around the cause of reaching every unreached people group on earth with the gospel of Jesus Christ. A Third of Us describes the unfinished task—the third of the world's population that hasn't had access to God's good news. I was honored to join host Greg Kelley, CEO of World Mission, to talk about the importance of leadership in indigenous movements and our response to global gospel poverty.

This conversation, entitled A Passion to Reach the Lost, first appeared on A Third of Us Podcast on November 12, 2021.
