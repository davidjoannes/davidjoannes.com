Sponsor Chinese Student For Christian Camp

April 24, 2013

Every May, we host our annual Within Reach Global English Camp for the college students and young professionals that have been attending our weekly English Corners throughout the year. In this camp they will learn to improve their English skills through group interaction, games, discussion and fellowship with other students and camp leaders. All throughout the camp program, the message of hope and love is shared. We take this opportunity to share the gospel and invite them to surrender their lives to God.

Check out past English Camps on Facebook:

We believe that this camp offers a meaningful and life changing experience for the attendees. They get a chance to hear about God, gain confidence in their English language abilities, and expand their potential for future employment. We believe students are positively impacted when they attend our camps.

This year, we are expecting over 100 college students to attend our camp on May 11-12.

We are hoping you will consider donating funds to help students who cannot afford to attend. Every year, we encounter students whose financial hardship excludes them from attending. With your help through sponsorship you can make a huge difference.

Although there are different types of sponsorship, we welcome any donation size.

Full camp sponsorship
$30 sponsors one student for an overnight stay. This includes the on campus stay in a dorm, 4 meals and all activities.

$30 for one student

Partial camp sponsorship
Partially sponsor one student for the amount of your choice.

Choose amount for  students 

From all of us at Within Reach Global, we represent the college students who will be impacted by your giving by saying thank you!
