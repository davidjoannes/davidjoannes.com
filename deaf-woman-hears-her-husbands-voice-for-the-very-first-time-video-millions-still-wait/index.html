Deaf Woman Hears Her Husbands Voice For The Very First Time [Video] Millions Still Wait

March 28, 2015

Imagine

what it would be like to live in a world completely silent,

devoid of sound,
muted music,
a constant hush.

You don’t even know what your loved ones’ voices sound like.



Deaf Woman Hears Her Husbands Voice For The Very First Time... Bless her!!

Posted by Tyrese Gibson on Thursday, March 26, 2015




After I saw this video of a deaf woman hearing her husband’s voice for the very first time, I began crying. There was something so touching about her listening to the articulations of a loved one for the first time in her life!

Now imagine

those who have never heard of Jesus before.

There’s more of them than you realize:

6,000+ unreached people groups
3,000+ unengaged ethnic tribes
2,000,000,000+ who have never heard the voice of

a missionary,
a Christian,
a pastor,
God.

What if we actually picked up and went to them? What if we cared so much about them that we began focusing our Christian evangelistic efforts in the 10/40 Window?

People are waiting at the other end of our obedience. They are waiting to hear of God’s salvation plan. Here’s a thought: Why should anyone have the right to hear the gospel twice when millions have never heard it once?

At Within Reach Global, we are passionate about taking the gospel to the least reached people groups of Southeast Asia. Will you help us help them?
Raise your voice for the voiceless today. Let your message of God’s love be heard for the very first time.
