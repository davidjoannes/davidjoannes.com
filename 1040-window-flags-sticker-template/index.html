Free Download: 10/40 Window Country Flags Sticker Template

March 23, 2022

Here's an awesome DIY idea for you to make the 10/40 Window and unreached people groups front and center in your family conversations. Every day, our family randomly selects a country to pray for in the 10/40 Window because we want to encourage a missionary mindset in our kids.

A lot of people have contacted us saying they want a tool to inspire their family members to join in prayer. Now you can navigate below to download the 10/40 Window Country Flags Sticker Template.

Why pray for unreached people in these countries? Because most of the people in the 10/40 Window don't have access to a church, Christian, missionary, or Bible. Learn more about the 10/40 Window and unreached people groups on the Within Reach Global website. And watch our three-minute promo video to understand how you and your family can directly impact unreached people groups with us:

So how do you make these 10/40 Window country flags? Start by downloading the 10/40 Window Country Flags Sticker Template:

[xyz-ihs snippet="1040-Window-Flag-Sticker-Template"]




What you'll need:

  • The 10/40 Window Country Flags Sticker Template

  • Transparent sticker paper (1 sheet)

  • Popsicle sticks (54 pieces)

  • A razor knife


Directions: Print all 54 10/40 Window country flags on transparent sticker paper, carefully cut out each flag (and its corresponding country name) and attach them to the popsicle sticks. Then pick one random country per day and pray for the gospel-deprived people in that country.

Your prayers will be enhanced when you watch a Prayercast video for specific prayer needs. Here's a direct link to the Prayercast YouTube channel. Simply search for the country you select each day. You can also visit prayercast.com to search by country.

Note: Within Reach Global's video podcast, Missions Pulse, has partnered together with Prayercast to inspire people to pray for the unreached. Subscribe to the Missions Pulse YouTube channel today.
