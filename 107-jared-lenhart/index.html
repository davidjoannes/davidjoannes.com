#107: A Missionary Legacy: Faith, Family, and the Fight to Reach Every Nation

April 29, 2026

Jared Lenhart is the president of Frontier Harvest Ministries, an organization committed to taking the Gospel of Jesus Christ to some of the most unreached and unengaged people groups across Asia. Jared talks about his family's legacy in missions, started by his parents, Chuck and Wendy Lenhart.

Learn more about Frontier Harvest Ministries at frontierharvest.com.
