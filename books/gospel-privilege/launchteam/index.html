FREE GIFT DETAILS

The Space Between Memories (Kindle Edition)

Save 90% on David Joannes' first book, The Space Between Memories, when you join the Book Launch Team. You'll be updated via email a few days after the release of Gospel Privilege on July 1. All you have to do is click the link I provide and you'll get The Space Between Memories (Kindle edition) for $0.99 cents.

Pray for ZERO Prayer Guide (PDF)

Pray for ZERO Prayer Guide: A Prayer Guide to Bring God's Word to All (2020) is a great resource to help you understand global gospel poverty. This prayer guide comes from The Seed Company's Pray for ZERO Movement. This resource is sure to move you with compassion for the gospel-deprived peoples of the earth.

Conscious Black Conservatism (E-Book)

Conscious Black Conservatism: Building Social, Political, and Economic Empires Based on Kingdom Principles by Felecia  Killings (editor of Gospel Privilege) is a 65-page e-book that teaches you why the key to our growth and success rests in our ability to apply spiritual laws to our lives.

"Into Every Nation" by Kyle Christensen (Music Single)

This mission-centric music single by All Nations Music & Kyle D. Christensen is sure to stir your passion for God's mission. Located in Chiang Mai, Thailand, Kyle Christensen is a full-time missionary and recording artist and the Founder of the All Nations House of Prayer (ANHOP), a worship and prayer ministry serving throughout Southeast Asia.

"Speak" by Kanita Benson (Music Single)

This powerful worship song from Christian recording artist Kanita Benson's "Surrender" EP will encourage and inspire you to hear God's voice amidst life's white noise. Kanita Benson, also the Founder of She Saves a Nation, appears in Gospel Privilege in  a chapter entitled "Gospel Compassion."

Exclusive Access to Video Interviews

You'll get exclusive access to video interviews of two important characters in Gospel Privilege. These up-close-and-personal conversations about their underlying motivations and behaviors will be a great addition to the stories you read about in the book.
