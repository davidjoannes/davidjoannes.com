MEET THE AUTHOR

David is the Founder/CEO of Within Reach Global and the author of The Space Between Memories (2016), The Mind of a Missionary (2018, 2024), The Mind of a Missionary (2024), Gospel Privilege (2021), See the City (2023), and the Glorious Hope coloring book (2023).

David has been involved in cross-cultural missionary work for two-and-a-half decades—15 years in China and 10 years in Thailand.

In 2008, David and his wife founded Within Reach Global Inc., a non-profit Christian mission organization focused on reaching unreached people groups in the 10/40 Window. He now oversees a growing number of foreign and indigenous missionary staff at Within Reach Global, headquartered in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

David is fluent in Mandarin Chinese and proficient in Tagalog and Thai. He lives in Chiang Mai, Thailand, with his wife and two daughters.
