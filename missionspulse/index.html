About the Missions Pulse Audience

The Missions Pulse audience is comprised of people who are interested in the missions venture. Some of them are serving as cross-cultural missionaries while others are involved in their local churches or serve on missions committees. Still others are seeking God about the next steps to take to fulfill their particular role in the Great Commission.

How the audience benefits from the show

We strongly believe that every citizen in God’s Kingdom is significant. Missions Pulse exists to help people recognize God’s missionary heart and join Him in the Great Commission call. We hope to provoke in our audience a missions zeal through encouraging interviews, inspiring stories, and quality missions resources.

How you as the guest benefit from the show

Through your interview, you will gain a broader reach among like-minded believers. You will also be able to share your interview with your partners and supporters. We will make the show available on all our social media channels, giving more credibility to you, your mission, and your vision. Ultimately, we desire to bring glory to God through each interview we do, inspiring every Christian to recognize their part to play in the Great Commission enterprise.

Logistics to join the show

Because we seek to provide a high-quality experience for our audience, we ask that you prepare accordingly before your interview. Please note that this is a video interview. You will need:


  • A quiet place to record.

  • High-speed internet access

  • All interviews will take place on the Riverside link that is automatically sent to the calendar event unless otherwise stated.

  • A desktop or laptop computer with either Chrome or Firefox browser installed. We recommend using your computer rather than your mobile device for higher-quality audio. If you have questions about microphones, please contact us for more information about our preferred devices.

What you need to prepare before the interview
  • We request your bio so that David can introduce you properly. Please include a brief description of yourself, your role in your ministry/organization, books you have authored, etc.
  • Please also provide us with your title/role description and ministry/organization name. This will be used in your lower thirds title when you appear on the screen.
  • Suggest a topic that you are passionate about. We want you to feel comfortable and speak on a topic that is relevant to your expertise.
  • Please send us a high-resolution photo of yourself. This may be used on our social media outlets.
