There's A Buzz About Reaching Unreached People Groups And Teams Are Coming

January 29, 2014

The buzz amongst churches and individuals from around the world has been growing over the many years that we have been living and serving in Southeast Asia. We believe that this year is a pivotal time in Within Reach Global’s growth.

Our schedule is packed. We have 6 teams from America and the Philippines joining us in China in March, April, May, June and July 2014! More people coming to help us reach unreached people groups in rural and urban settings means many more souls being ushered into the kingdom. It blesses us to be conduits of God’s glory to places where the name of Jesus has never gone before.

Visiting teams will be helping us at our English Corners among the 100,000 college students at The Bridge outreach center. They will be pouring their lives into discipling young people who want to know more about Jesus. They will also be visiting Love For The Poor's Father’s House, our partner orphanage/migrant children’s home, serving local missionaries, and doing intercessory prayer walks through the city.

Can you see the incredible things God is doing? There is only 1 missionary for every 278,431 people in the 10/40 Window, where we live and work...
