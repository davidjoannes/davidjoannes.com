Missions Pulse 5: Brian Britton

February 4, 2019

The Global South is a term that refers to what may also be called the "Developing World" (i.e., Africa, Latin America, and the developing countries in Asia).

Young people from the Global South—in this instance, specifically Brazil—are rising up with a missions fervor to finish the task of bringing the Gospel to every part of the planet.

In this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Brian Britton about a growing missions fervor in the global south. Listen in as we talk about a passion for missions rising from places that were formally the mission fields of the world. Brian is the senior pastor of The Dwelling Place Church in Richmond, VA and serves as a part of the Family Care team with IRIS Global.

The words of John Piper remind us of God's global plan that is inclusive of all nations: “God is pursuing with omnipotent passion a worldwide purpose of gathering joyful worshipers for Himself from every tribe and tongue and people and nation. He has an inexhaustible enthusiasm for the supremacy of His name among the nations. Therefore, let us bring our affections into line with His, and, for the sake of His name, let us renounce the quest for worldly comforts and join His global purpose.”
