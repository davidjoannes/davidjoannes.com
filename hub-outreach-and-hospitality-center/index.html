Here's An Awesome Ministry To Sow Into And Help Reach Young People In China

April 7, 2014

Over the last 5 years, we have directly impacted over 1,000 Chinese college students at The Hub outreach center!

we have directly impacted over 1,000 Chinese college students at The Hub outreach center!

Right now at The Hub Outreach & Hospitality Center:


  • nearly 100college students and young professionals attend our English Corner every week,

  • 40 are being discipled every Saturday,

  • 6 were just recently baptized,

  • and 20 have joined ministry outreaches among orphans and underprivileged children.


The Hub Outreach & Hospitality Center is a huge conduit of God’s blessing in China! It is a place where

  • young people come to know God in an intimate way,

  • local missionaries and visiting teams have a short term place to stay,

  • and where local Chinese believers gather for Church every week.


“The Hub outreach center has not only helped me to speak English, but it has shown me that God has a plan for my life.” - *James

The Hub outreach center has shown me that God has a plan for my life!

We just moved to a brand new location in the heart of the city, and for the last 2 weeks, our dedicated Within Reach Global staff have been repairing, cleaning, painting and preparing the new center for an influx of new target students.

In the last month, 3 volunteer short term teams have come to help grow this ministry. There is one team here in China with us right now. 3 more are coming in the next 3 months.

We are amazed at the rapid growth of this ministry!

Now we need your help.

Our monthly operating cost is $1,300, which covers everything from


  • monthly rent

  • utilities

  • management fee

  • Discipleship Dinners

  • local missionary expenses


We stepped out in faith without the promise of financial help, and set up this new outreach center, because we are passionate about reaching people who have little to no understanding of the gospel. Our passion is to honor God by reaching unreached people groups, and we want to ask for your help to bring the message of salvation to those who have yet to hear and understand it.
"After I got saved and was discipled at The Hub, I was able to lead my mother to Jesus as well! I am so happy that God is changing my family!” - *Jennifer

Right now we have enough support to operate until mid June, 2014. After that, we need to find the finances to continue reaching these college students.

Would you please pray about donating to The Hub Outreach & Hospitality Center?

Any amount will help bring the gospel message to a lost and seeking generation in China.

Would you pray about making a monthly donation of $25, $50, $75 or $100? Why not inspire your church or small group to give $1,300 to impact souls for one whole month? And, of course, your donation of any selected amount will bring the message of grace and salvation to young Chinese men and women.

We are so thrilled to partner with God by reaching this generational of China’s future leaders with the gospel of Jesus Christ! We pray that you too will be inspired to join us in this exciting journey.
