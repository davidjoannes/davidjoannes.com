Missions Pulse 61: Kyle Christensen

March 17, 2020

On this episode of Missions Pulse, David Joannes interviews Kyle Christensen, founder of All Nations House of Prayer, a member of the leadership team at YWAM CM in relation to Call2All, co-founder of OneEleven Global, and former staff at International House of Prayer.

Kyle Christensen talks about navigating worldly culture with Kingdom culture in mind, indigenous expressions of worship, the sustaining power of prayer and unity, how God communicates His mission to us through worship and prayer, and how to not miss the plot and become disengaged from God’s mission.

When we stop talking to God, we miss the plot and become disengaged from God's mission. He desires that every nation, tongue, tribe, and people worship Him. But worship does not arise from many pockets of the earth. Gospel deprivation is a real thing, and we have not made it a paramount conversation in the Church. Kyle Christensen says that revival is simply people talking to God and doing what He says. Are you ready to spend time with Him and follow His leading?

Prayer and unity sustain us on the cross-cultural mission field. There are a lot of nuances that can be difficult to navigate. We need to have our ears fine-tuned to God's voice and walk in Christian unity to see His Kingdom come.

We want to see the Kingdom of God permeate every small kingdom of the world. This should be a big conversation, but oftentimes it gets pushed to the sidelines. It can be difficult to decipher the difference between the culture of the Kingdom and the cultures of the world. Pray that God's reign usurps all the menial things and He is lifted up in our midst.

Prayer is partnership with God. Prayer is, in fact, the mighty engine that is to move the missionary work. God is always speaking and communicating His mission to us. How can you hear His voice? By spending time with Him in prayer and worship. This, then, leads to joining God in His overarching mission to establish His Kingdom on the earth.
