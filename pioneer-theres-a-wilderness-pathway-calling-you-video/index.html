Pioneer, There's A Wilderness Pathway Calling You [Video]

November 2, 2014

Pioneer. The lyrics of this song have been revolving in my mind, whispered upon my lips, for the last week:

Pioneer, pioneer
Keep pressing onward, beyond your fear
Only the Father goes before you
To your own frontier
You're a pioneer

Pioneer Keep pressing onward beyond your fear. Only the Father goes before you to your own frontier You're a pioneer

As the founder of Within Reach Global, my passion has always been to go where there is no path and make a trail.

"Do not go where the path may lead. Go instead where there is no path and make a trail." - Ralph Waldo Emerson

Our anthem at Within Reach Global is that of a trailblazer. We cannot help but go where the zeros are: zero churches, zero Christians, zero missionaries, zero chance of hearing the gospel of Jesus but for our obedience and willingness.

As I lead a group of pioneer missionaries in Southeast Asia, my heart is to see them to greater things than I will ever accomplish.

What you have done others will do
Bigger and better, and faster than you
But you can't look back, you gotta keep pressing through
There's a wilderness pathway, calling you

Nancy Honeytree wrote Pioneer during the Jesus Movement. Rick Pino has recently revived the song. Here are the lyrics. I pray you too are inspired to join us in bringing the gospel to those who have yet to hear.
